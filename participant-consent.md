### Summary of Experimental Methods & Psychometric Measures
* **Cohort & Procedure:** ~50 professionals across public and institutional sectors grouped into 10-person experimental markets for 60–90 minute mixed-methods sessions.
* **Economic Experimental Games (EEGs):** Dictator Game (baseline altruism), Ultimatum Game (fairness/rejection thresholds), Trust Game (investor-trustee reciprocity), Prisoner's Dilemma (cooperation vs. defection), Public Goods Game (common pool contributions), and Commons Dilemma (resource depletion).
* **Psychometric Instruments:**
  * **HEXACO-PI-R:** Honesty-Humility (sincerity, fairness, greed avoidance) and Agreeableness vs. Anger (forgiveness vs. negative reciprocity).
  * **IPIP-NEO:** Trust (belief in the good intentions of others) and Excitement-Seeking (risk tolerance).
  * **Facet MAP:** Altruism & Fairness (pro-social motives), Extrospection (Theory of Mind/strategic analysis), and Risk-Aversion (direct risk preferences).

### Invitation to Participate in a Research Experiment: How We Collectively Solve Shared Resource Management Problems

#### What is this research about?
This research aims to understand how groups of people make decisions when they share resources, like public funds or important information. We also want to explore how different rules and ways of sharing information might help people work together better to manage these shared resources.

#### What will I be asked to do?
In the initial phase of this research, you will be asked to participate in interviews. Participants will be asked to make decisions regarding the allocation of resources. The information gathered from these interviews will be used to help us understand real-world decision-making. This information will then be used to create simulations of markets with the aim of understanding how different rules and ways of sharing information might help people work together better to manage shared resources.

#### How long will it take and what will I be paid?
The duration of the interviews will vary, but we anticipate each interview session to last approximately **60–90 minutes**. Participation in this study is completely voluntary, and the information collected will remain confidential. You will be paid a show-up fee of **$10** plus a reward amount that will be calculated based on your overall performance in the experiment up to **$150**. Therefore, the maximum payout is **$160** (= $10 show-up fee plus reward based on performance).

#### What are the benefits of participating?
By participating in this research, you will be contributing to a better understanding of how people manage shared resources in Australia. Our findings could help:

* Improve the efficiency and sustainability of public services.
* Inform the development of better government policies.
* Lead to new ways of designing systems that encourage cooperation and trust in shared resource management.

Your insights will be instrumental in shaping the virtual environments we create for our simulations, providing a foundation for future experiments with real people.

#### Are there any risks involved?
We have carefully considered any potential challenges or risks associated with your participation:

* **Emotional Discomfort:** Some of the decision-making scenarios (Economic Games) and personality questions may touch on sensitive topics related to honesty, fairness, and personal risk-taking, which could cause a temporary feeling of discomfort or mild emotional stress.
* **Conflict of Interest:** If you are a student, your grades will not be affected by your decision to participate, decline, or withdraw from this project at any stage.
* **Informed Consent:** Before you participate, we will provide you with a comprehensive consent form outlining exactly what information we will collect, how it will be used, how it will be kept safe, any potential privacy risks, and your right to withdraw at any time.
* **De-identification:** We will rigorously remove any names or other identifying information from your data as soon as possible. The information you provide will be used to calibrate the characteristics and behaviours of computer-based participants in our simulations, not to identify you personally.
* **Secure Storage and Restricted Access:** All data will be stored securely on University-hosted servers, protected by passwords and other safeguards. Only approved members of the research team will have access to the de-identified data.
* **Withdrawal of Data:** You have the right to withdraw your consent and any of the data you have provided at any time, for any reason, without penalty. Once the data has been de-identified and combined with the results from other participants for analysis, it will no longer be possible to identify and remove your specific information.
* **Extended Consent for Future Research Use:** We are asking for your consent to use the de-identified data and information collected during this project for future research studies conducted by the research team or other qualified researchers. This ensures that the valuable insights from this study can contribute to the broader scientific community. Be assured that any data shared for future research will be completely stripped of any identifying information (de-identified) and used only for ethical and approved research purposes.
* **Transcription of Interviews:** All recorded interviews will be transcribed to convert the spoken content into text for analysis. These transcriptions will be carried out using Microsoft Teams as a University-approved transcription service. The transcripts will be de-identified and reviewed for accuracy by a member of the research team before they are used for the study's analysis.

#### Will I hear about the results of this project?
Yes, we are committed to sharing our findings. Once the research is complete, and the results are analysed and published, we will make a summary of the key findings available on our project website. This will be an opportunity for you to see the impact of your contribution.
